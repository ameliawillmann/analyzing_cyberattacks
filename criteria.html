Limitations

Lack of Information!

The public information about cyberattacks on critical infrastructure is limited because everyone is choosing if and when they want to report the cyberattack. Companies are not being held responsible for informing their clients and customers of data breaches. Additionally, government agencies strategically decide when they admit they’ve been hacked or take responsibility for an attack. It is the game of geopolitics. For example, Chinese Hacktivists will hack the Taiwanese government (as it frequently does), Taiwan will blame China for the attack, China will deny responsibility, and the cycle continues. On occasion, global powers will hint at certain attacks, usually bragging about its nation’s capabilities to incite fear.

Overall, information sharing about cybersecurity is complicated because of the risk publicly exposing vulnerabilities poses. The reporting and responsibility blaming and claiming is purely strategic between foreign entities and adversaries on the international stage.
