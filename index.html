About this Capstone

Welcome to my Capstone Project! My name is Amelia Willmann, and I am a Northeastern University student studying Data Science & International Affairs. My passion lies at the intersection of these two fields, specifically the push-and-pull of emerging tech on geopolitical dynamics. This curiosity introduced me to the Cybersecurity field ... sparking my interest in cyberattacks and their usage in modern warfare. For the feasibility of this project, I have attempted to narrow my focus to cyberattacks on critical infrastructure, although this is also a broad topic. Rather than tackle everything there is to know about cyberattacks on critical infrastructure, I hope to instead draw your attention to a topic that is not receiving enough attention in the news.
